# Newton: what users and the documentation already report

Gathered 15 October 2026, before the deep dive's registered studies, to aim the first tests. Every item is a report
or a reading of Newton's code; each links to its source. The last column says what this record has since measured.
N16 to N20 were added on 19 October 2026 from a second search of the tracker: the issues filed since, and the open
pull requests, which are measured in [the fixes already out there](fixes-in-the-wild.md).

## The first tests this points at

1. The slide that ends, on SemiImplicit, Featherstone, XPBD, VBD and the MuJoCo Warp solver (N1 is the cheapest and
   most severe item to confirm or refute).
2. The hold and a grip, per solver (N2).
3. Limits: scaled pendulums striking their limits, and an XPBD range beyond plus or minus pi (N5, N6).
4. A free body with its centre of mass off its origin, and a multi-link arm (N8).
5. Parity and determinism on the CPU: one cube drop on MuJoCo C 3.16, Newton's MuJoCo Warp solver on Warp's CPU
   device, repeated (N9, N10, N11).
