# What was found in Newton

Newton 1.6.1 as released for F1 to F17, and 1.7.0 from F18 (on 1.7.0 every SemiImplicit, Featherstone and XPBD play
of F1 to F17 replays byte for byte, and VBD's but its joint past pi, which 1.7.0's winding fix changes:
[Study 7](studies/07-newton-1.7.0.md)), on Warp's CPU device, each solver with its own defaults. Every number is from a
run in [`validation/`](../validation/), read by the Senua AI Robotics Core's measuring programs. Design probes find
where to look; a pre-registered study decides. The table says which.

## What Newton gets right

- **Deterministic to the byte** on the CPU: each of the six solvers played gave the same bytes when the hold was played again (run 001), and XPBD's whole ten-test suite, played again, is the same to the byte (run 016).
- **Free fall** at gravity's rate on every solver but VBD at every step played (run 001), and on XPBD at every height played, 0.15 m to 10 m (run 006).
- **The ground carries the weight** exactly on every solver (run 001).
- **XPBD and the MuJoCo solver give Coulomb's friction** on a slide and over an impact (runs 001, 003, 004).
- **The MuJoCo solver plays as MuJoCo's C engine does**: the same creep at rest, the same slide (run 001).
- **Joint chains under Featherstone and the MuJoCo solver play as MuJoCo's C engine does**, to the part per million, and the MuJoCo solver's RK4 keeps a free chain's energy and momentum to 5 ppm (run 014).
